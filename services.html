Risk measurement

Hive Risk Ladder™

Every asset is classified into one of five zones. The zone decides which block it can enter, how large the position can be and how often it is reviewed.

  • Green Hive Zone™ · Ultra-Stable FoundationCan enter SEED Block™ and FLOW Block™.
  • Lime Hive Zone™ · Proven Conviction AssetsFit for CORE Block™, and conservative YIELD Block™.
  • Amber Hive Zone™ · Emerging LeadersBelongs in INNOVATION Block™, with YIELD Block™ where suitable.
  • Orange Hive Zone™ · High-Risk OpportunitiesINNOVATION Block™ in smaller, capped positions.
  • Red Hive Zone™ · Speculative Edge CasesLimited INNOVATION Block™, strictly capped and watched closely.

The higher the zone, the tighter the limits and the more frequent the monitoring.

Risk Ladder Severity™

Each holding also receives a Risk Ladder Severity™ from 1 to 5: its zone, read against your own portfolio and knowledge. The same asset can carry a different severity for different people.

Pre-set responses

Each zone has triggers defined in advance. When one is reached, the response is already chosen:

  1. Monitor
  2. Trim (take profit)
  3. De-risk
  4. Exit
  5. Freeze
Cash flow routing

Hive Flow Routes™

When a pre-set response reduces part of a position, the proceeds already have a destination. Decided in advance, when you are calm, before the move. Two routing maps cover both cases:

Take Profit RoutingWhen a position reaches its target
  • Liquidity → into the FLOW Block™
  • Reinvest → into the CORE Block™
  • New Asset → into the INNOVATION Block™
  • Personal → withdrawn to you
Exit & Defense RoutingWhen you reduce a position to protect the portfolio
  • Safety Reserve → into the FLOW Block™
  • Recovery Position → a low-risk CORE Block™ position
  • Core Consolidation → strengthens the CORE Block™

Hive Flow Routes™ give every sale a destination: a system for what happens after the decision.

THE HIVE BLOCK STRATEGY™

Most people in crypto are still looking for a clear structure, whether they are just starting out or already holding assets. With the Hive Block Strategy™ you learn to organise your holdings across five functional blocks, each with a distinct purpose and logic. With structure in place, you decide before the market does.

SEED Block™Security Foundation

CORE Block™Conviction Holdings

YIELD Block™Income Generation

  • SEED Block™ · Security Foundation. Stable assets and custody: the anchor, activated first.
  • CORE Block™ · Conviction Holdings. Long-term foundation: your key assets and their role.
  • FLOW Block™ · Operational Liquidity. Stablecoins, fiat and dry powder: act with intent.
  • INNOVATION Block™ · Asymmetric Opportunity. Thesis-driven positions with measured exposure.
  • YIELD Block™ · Income Generation. Staking and income, when market and readiness align.

Each block follows clear rules, timeframes and monitoring logic. Every position has a reason.

SEED Block™ is always activated first. INNOVATION Block™ opens once the foundational structure is confirmed. YIELD Block™ is activated when market coherence and your readiness both meet defined thresholds.

Knowing the zone defines key decisions: allocation, review frequency and action triggers. You learn to apply this framework consistently, with decisions set in advance, in calm conditions.

HQEA™ — OUR ANALYSIS ENGINE

HIVE QUANTUM ENHANCED Algorithm™

The architecture of personalised crypto strategy

Market analysis usually describes the market. The HQEA™ goes one step further: it shows what the market means for you. It is the analysis engine of Uncrypto The Crypto™, operated by Uncrypto The Crypto™ by Borghi Consulting™, and it puts the market and the person on one map.

Square one

Market Map

The analysis side of the HQEA™: our own native system, developed in-house and consolidated through continuous use. It reads every asset through four analytical pillars, whose weight shifts as conditions change.

  • Fundamentals: the economics and macro backdrop
  • On-Chain: what the network and its holders are actually doing
  • Technical: price structure, volatility and momentum
  • Sentiment & Narrative: positioning and the story driving attention

Result: the Hive Quantum Score™, the market regime and Base, Bull and Bear scenarios.

Built with Make Crypto Make Sense™, our own native system. Its core is the Hive Risk Profile™: a structured questionnaire that profiles you across several dimensions, then checked against your real portfolio through the Hive Portfolio Tool™.

  • Knowledge Index: what you understand today
  • Strategy Readiness Index: how ready you are to act on a plan
  • Hive Archetype™: your decision style and dominant bias
