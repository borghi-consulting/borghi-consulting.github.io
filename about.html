Our story

WHY WE EXIST

Most people in crypto do not lack information. They lack structure.

They hold assets they only partly understand, make decisions based on noise rather than conviction, and follow voices that disappear the moment markets turn. A framework, a plan and someone in their corner are what is missing.

That is the problem Uncrypto The Crypto™ by Borghi Consulting™ was built to solve.

To understand why, it helps to know how we got here.

Next: Alessandro →
Co-Founder

ALESSANDRO

Alessandro had spent years at the highest levels of business strategy, consulting, development and entrepreneurship. He entered crypto the way most people do, through a trusted friend: a platform, a promise, a narrative that sounded solid. He put money in and lost everything.

Trying to recover, he did what anyone would and tried again. Another platform, more people around him doing the same. He lost again.

Then came the gurus, the influencers and the narratives. He did not just follow them, he believed them, and ended up with a portfolio full of tokens worth next to nothing, high exposure and still no real direction.

“What struck him was more than the loss. It was the absence of any real framework in a space moving billions of dollars and leaving people completely exposed. Nobody was building the structure this space desperately needed.”
Next: Giulia →
Co-Founder

GIULIA

Giulia came from a different world entirely. She was a digital creative, and for years she watched her work disappear into platforms that profited from it while the people who made it saw almost nothing in return.

When NFTs emerged, they felt like an answer: a structural shift, a space where creators could finally own what they built and where the value of the work could return to the person who made it.

She came in because she believed the idea was right, and for a while it was. Then it collapsed. The tokens became worthless, the rewards disappeared, and the community that had felt real scattered without warning.

“She had seen how a space can feel open and transparent while being built to obscure: the language that welcomes people in is the same language that ensures they never fully understand what they are walking into.”
